Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data OpenTenant processes when you use our Terraform and Kubernetes IDE, why we process it, and the controls you have—especially when connecting to third‑party cloud providers.

1. Who we are

OpenTenant (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates opentenant.org and provides a Terraform and Kubernetes integrated development environment (IDE) that connects to multiple hosting providers (such as AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, and others) so that platform teams can design, deploy, and operate isolated tenant environments.

For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the data controller is OpenTenant LLC, registered at [REGISTERED ADDRESS]. Our data protection contact is privacy@opentenant.org.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the data needed to run the service. The categories below reflect what OpenTenant actually processes when you use the website and the IDE.

2.1 Account and identity data

When you sign up or log in, we collect your name, email address, organisation name, and authentication identifiers. If you use single sign‑on (SSO), we receive the identifiers provided by your identity provider (such as SAML NameID, which is typically an email address).

2.2 Infrastructure and workspace data

To provide the IDE, we store the Terraform configurations, Kubernetes manifests, workspace definitions, and deployment logs that you create. Terraform state files are treated as highly sensitive because they can contain resource attributes, generated passwords, and keys[reference:2]. We encrypt state at rest and restrict access according to workspace roles.

2.3 Cloud provider connections

When you connect a cloud provider, you may supply credentials (for example, AWS access keys, GCP service account keys, or Azure service principals). We handle provider credentials with isolation: they are encrypted immediately, injected only into ephemeral worker processes during a Terraform run, and never persisted in plaintext or exposed back to the browser[reference:3]. Where supported, we encourage the use of dynamic, short‑lived credentials (such as OIDC‑based tokens) instead of static keys[reference:4].

2.4 Technical and log data

We automatically collect IP address, browser type, device information, and timestamps when you interact with the website and IDE. These logs help us secure the service, diagnose errors, and understand usage patterns. We redact known secret values from logs before storage[reference:5].

2.5 Cookies and analytics

We use strictly necessary cookies for authentication and session management. We also use a privacy‑respecting analytics tool to measure aggregate page views; this tool does not set advertising cookies and does not track you across third‑party sites. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

3. How we use information

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate the service — authenticating you, storing your workspaces, running Terraform plans and applies, and displaying logs.
  • To secure the platform — detecting abuse, preventing unauthorised access, and maintaining audit trails of administrative actions and deployments[reference:6].
  • To improve the product — analysing aggregated usage trends to prioritise features and fix bugs.
  • To communicate with you — sending service notices, security alerts, and responding to support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations — retaining records where required by law and responding to lawful requests.

4. Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the UK, we rely on the following lawful bases:

  • Contract — processing account and workspace data is necessary to perform our agreement with you.
  • Legitimate interests — securing the platform, preventing fraud, and improving the service, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — retaining records when required by applicable law.
  • Consent — where we ask for your consent (for example, for optional analytics), you may withdraw it at any time.

5. Sharing and subprocessors

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with service providers that help us operate OpenTenant, under written data‑processing agreements. Current subprocessors include:

  • Cloud hosting providers — for compute, storage, and managed databases (e.g., AWS, GCP, or Azure).
  • Authentication and SSO providers — to verify identity when you log in.
  • Monitoring and error‑reporting tools — to detect outages and diagnose failures.
  • Email delivery services — to send transactional messages such as password resets and security alerts.

A current list of subprocessors, including their locations, is available on request. We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of OpenTenant, our users, or the public.

6. International transfers

OpenTenant is operated from Germany. Some subprocessors may process data outside your country. Where we transfer personal data from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland to a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or the UK Addendum.

7. Security

We implement technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data and infrastructure secrets:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES‑256 or equivalent) for sensitive data, including provider credentials and Terraform state[reference:7].
  • Ephemeral credential injection: secrets are decrypted only in memory inside isolated workers and are never written to disk or exposed to the web UI[reference:8].
  • Role‑based access control (RBAC) that scopes workspace, state, and credential access to authorised users[reference:9].
  • Immutable audit logs for administrative changes and deployment events[reference:10].

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe your interaction with OpenTenant is no longer secure, please contact us immediately at security@opentenant.org.

8. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account data — retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward to allow reactivation and comply with legal obligations.
  • Workspace and state data — retained according to your plan and workspace settings; you can delete workspaces at any time, after which data is removed from active systems within 1 year.
  • Logs — technical and audit logs are retained for 1 year for security and diagnostic purposes.

When retention periods expire, we delete or anonymise the data in accordance with our internal deletion procedures.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Restriction — limit how we process your data in certain circumstances.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine‑readable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@opentenant.org. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

California residents: you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. OpenTenant does not sell personal information. To exercise your CCPA/CPRA rights, contact us at the email above.

10. Cookies

We use a minimal set of cookies. Essential cookies are required for login and security; they cannot be disabled without breaking the service. Analytics cookies, if used, are configured to respect “Do Not Track” signals and do not collect cross‑site identifiers. A detailed cookie table is available in our Cookie Policy.

11. Children’s privacy

OpenTenant is a business‑oriented platform and is not directed to individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by email or by posting a prominent notice on the website before the change takes effect. The “Effective” date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.
